01Our story

Built for people who show up.

Oxygen opened in 2016 with one squat rack, a borrowed sound system, and a simple rule: nobody trains alone. Ten years later the equipment is newer and the music is louder, but the rule hasn't changed.

No contracts that trap you. No “secret” supplements. Just honest coaching, clean equipment, and a room full of people who keep showing up — especially on the days it's hard. Whether you're here to lose your first 5 kg or add 20 to your squat, there's a spot for you.

Manish Chauhan Founder & head coach
